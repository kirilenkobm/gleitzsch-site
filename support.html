Web photo editor

Open the editor on a computer with a WebGPU-capable browser. Your photograph is processed on your device; it is not uploaded.

  1. Open or drop a JPEG or PNG. Random is selected by default and starts exploring when your photo opens.
  2. Choose a preset or use Custom to adjust the mix. Melt finds an edge and lets it flow downward. In Custom → Melt, Line height prefers a natural edge near the height you choose; Auto returns to automatic selection. Speed changes how quickly live effects move.
  3. Use Text and choose Tape or Plain. Plain has no background and lets you choose the text color. Drag the outline to move text and use Size and Angle to adjust it. Exposure changes brightness.
  4. Pause to hold a moment, then Save frame to download a separate PNG. Original compares the untouched photo. Hold Rewind during Random to revisit up to three previous mixes; release to keep the displayed mix in Custom.
  5. Choose Record video, then Stop & download to save a silent MP4/H.264 clip. Effects, Random, text, Speed and Pause remain usable. Recording stops at 10 minutes, the encoded-size limit, or when you hide the tab. Download video again retries the latest clip until you start another recording or leave the page. This needs browser support for H.264 recording.

Recording keeps the photo and dimensions fixed: opening another photo, rotation, Original and PNG saving wait until it finishes. The encoded-data limit is 256 MiB, with an earlier stop to leave room for finalization. Closing the page before it finishes discards the unfinished clip.

Saving uses the working resolution shown below the preview. Presets and Reset effects keep your text and exposure; a new photo clears them. Saved custom slots, video import and automatic project saving are not available in this web version.

After the editor has loaded, editing and saving can continue without a network connection. Closing or reloading clears the current edit. See the web editor privacy policy. For a problem, include your browser, operating system and version/build from About → Technical details.

Install the Android alpha

The Android build is an early test version shared directly by the developer. It is not available through Google Play yet. More polished builds and Play testing are next.

  1. On an ARM64 phone running Android 10 or later, download the 4.10.1 APK (29.5 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow this browser or file app to install unknown apps, then install Gleitzsch.
  3. Open Gleitzsch and allow camera access when you want to use the camera. Save results to your phone’s photo library.

Only Nothing Phone (3a) has been tested so far. Video is silent, Pixelate can be slow, and some iOS features are still missing. The classic photo picker on older Android versions may ask you to confirm your selection.

These early builds may need reinstalling when store testing starts. Keep copies of recipes you care about using Custom → heart → Copy current recipe. Photos and videos you have saved belong to your phone’s media library.

To update, download the newer APK and install it over your existing Gleitzsch app without uninstalling it. Your saved mixes stay in the app.

For a problem report, include your phone model, Android version and Gleitzsch version. Use GitHub Issues or the support email below. There is no automatic update service in this APK.

Report a problem

Use the public GitHub issue tracker for bugs, questions and ideas. You’ll need a GitHub account to post. Search existing issues first — someone may have found the same thing. Issues aren’t only for things that broke — ideas for another way to mess the picture up belong here too.

Email support

For support or privacy questions, email gleitzsch@mail.online-impressum.de. You can also use Contact support in the app’s About screen. It opens a draft with the app and operating system versions; nothing is sent automatically. If no mail app is available, copy the address.

Only include photographs or attachments you want to share with the developer. For public bug reports and ideas, GitHub Issues remains available.

What helps

  • Your phone model and operating system version (iOS or Android).
  • The Gleitzsch version and build number from ⓘ → About.
  • What you did, what happened and what you expected.
  • The preset or copied recipe values, if the problem concerns a particular effect.

Attach a screenshot only if you’re comfortable sharing everything visible in it.

TestFlight feedback (iOS)

Once Apple graciously allows me to offer a TestFlight beta in the first place.

If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple’s TestFlight app. This avoids posting it to the public issue tracker. Apple supplies submitted feedback and available diagnostics to the developer under its TestFlight privacy terms.

Have an idea?

If you know another way to mess the picture up, suggest it. A reference helps: a still, a clip or a link you’re comfortable leaving public. You can also describe a part of the camera that feels awkward. This is a small personal project, so replies and changes may take a little time.

Quick start

A few essentials for the phone app.

Choose your starting point
Use the camera, or open Menu → Photo Editor to choose a picture.
Find a look
Let Random explore, choose a preset, or open Custom to adjust the effects. In Custom, choose an effect group, select a setting and adjust it on the scale. Presets stays open while you compare looks; close it with the cross, a downward swipe on its header or a tap outside.
Catch a mix
Hold the rewind button during Random to revisit up to three previous mixes. Release to keep the shown mix in Custom. Hold the eye button to compare the untouched image. Random speed reaches 120%; Menu also contains Keep Screen On and About.
Add text
Tap Aa and choose Tape or Plain. Plain keeps letter case and emoji, with your choice of text color and no background. Move, resize or rotate text; both styles go through the effects too.
Save a moment
Tap the shutter for a photo. Hold it to start a video, then tap again to stop. In the editor, save a frame as a new PNG. Your original stays unchanged.
Keep a favourite
In Custom, tap the heart to save a mix to C1, C2 or C3, or choose Copy current recipe.
