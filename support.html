Install the Android alpha

The Android build is an early test version shared directly by the developer. It is not available through Google Play yet. More polished builds and Play testing are next.

  1. On an ARM64 phone running Android 10 or later, download the 4.8.0 APK (29.2 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow this browser or file app to install unknown apps, then install Gleitzsch.
  3. Open Gleitzsch and allow camera access when you want to use the camera. Save results to your phone’s photo library.

Only Nothing Phone (3a) has been tested so far. Video is silent, Pixelate can be slow, and some iOS features are still missing. The classic photo picker on older Android versions may ask you to confirm your selection.

These early builds may need reinstalling when store testing starts. Keep copies of recipes you care about using Recipe values → Copy. Photos and videos you have saved belong to your phone’s media library.

For a problem report, include your phone model, Android version and Gleitzsch version. Use GitHub Issues or the support email below. There is no automatic update service in this APK.

Report a problem

Use the public GitHub issue tracker for bugs, questions and ideas. You’ll need a GitHub account to post. Search existing issues first — someone may have found the same thing. Issues aren’t only for things that broke — ideas for another way to mess the picture up belong here too.

Issues and attachments are public. Do not post private photographs, email addresses, payment details or other sensitive information.

Email support

For support or privacy questions, email bogdan.kirilenko+gleitzsch@proton.me. You can also use Contact support in the app’s About screen. It opens a draft with the app and operating system versions; nothing is sent automatically. If no mail app is available, copy the address.

Only include photographs or attachments you want to share with the developer. For public bug reports and ideas, GitHub Issues remains available.

What helps

  • Your phone model and operating system version (iOS or Android).
  • The Gleitzsch version and build number from ⓘ → About.
  • What you did, what happened and what you expected.
  • The preset or copied recipe values, if the problem concerns a particular effect.

Attach a screenshot only if you’re comfortable sharing everything visible in it.

TestFlight feedback (iOS)

Once Apple graciously allows me to offer a TestFlight beta in the first place.

If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple’s TestFlight app. This avoids posting it to the public issue tracker. Apple supplies submitted feedback and available diagnostics to the developer under its TestFlight privacy terms.

Have an idea?

If you know another way to mess the picture up, suggest it. A reference helps: a still, a clip or a link you’re comfortable leaving public. You can also describe a part of the camera that feels awkward. This is a small personal project, so replies and changes may take a little time.

Quick start

The controls below describe the shared Gleitzsch experience. Android is still a work in progress; some features may not yet be available in development builds. See current availability.

Edit photo
Tap Edit photo beside the Gleitzsch shutter and choose a photograph. Use Random, Presets or Custom, just as in the camera. Tap Horizontal or Vertical above the image to switch the glitch direction. Wide photos start Horizontal. Tall and square photos start Vertical. On iOS, open Custom in the photo editor, then Compression, and try MSV1 error. Each new photo starts at 0%. Random keeps the amount you set. Original lets you compare. Saving pauses the effect and keeps the displayed frame as a separate PNG in your photo library. Tap Continue for another variation. If a photo is too large to open, choose a smaller copy.
Open from Photos (iOS)
In Apple Photos, share one picture and choose Edit in Gleitzsch to open it immediately. Save to Photos adds a separate PNG. Close returns to Photos. This share-sheet editor has its own C1–C3 mixes.
Random
The camera starts in Random unless a C1–C3 favorite was active when you last used it. Random keeps running when you rotate the phone and picks up where it left off when you return to the app.
Presets
Choose Iridescence, VHS, Old TV, Broken Air, Echo Chamber or Pixelate, or a saved C1–C3 mix. In the camera, direction follows your phone: Vertical in portrait, Horizontal in landscape. Rotating keeps the selected preset. TV and CRT lines continue to follow the display.
Custom
Open Custom to see the current settings. From Random, this keeps the current mix. An active preset stays selected until you change a setting. Tap Save preset to save a mix; use the saved-slot menu to replace or delete it. Reset clears the adjustments. Swipe the camera’s Custom panel down to close it, or use the close button in the editor.
Stripes
In Custom, turn the image into bright lines that bend with its details. Stripes controls the strength; Stripe density changes their spacing. Random can explore this effect too. Copy and saved C1–C3 mixes keep both settings.
Echo train
In Custom, Echo train adds fading ghosts on both sides along the effect direction. Comb spacing sets their distance.
Barrel
In Custom, gently bulge the image while keeping the rectangular frame filled, or set it to 0 for a flat picture. It works in both directions, with the same result in the preview, photographs and videos.
Pixelate
Choose Pixelate for a pixelated look. You can adjust the pixel size and add other effects in Custom. Starting Random turns pixelation off.
Screen
The lock button beside the shutter keeps the screen awake while the camera is open. Tap it again to let the screen sleep. It starts off each time you open Gleitzsch.
Tape text
Tap Aa in the camera or photo editor to add white text on black tape. Drag a tape to move it. Select a tape, then use two fingers anywhere on the image to resize, turn and move it together. Double-tap to edit; select a tape and tap the trash to delete it. + adds another tape; Done returns to normal controls. Tape takes the image effects and appears in saved photos and video. Arrange it before recording. Presets and Random keep your text. Camera text lasts for the app session; editor text clears when you change photo or leave the editor.
Focus and exposure
Tap a subject in the camera preview to focus. Drag the sun up for a brighter image or down for a darker one. The adjustment ranges from −3 to +3 EV and works for photos and video. Double-tap the sun to reset. Exposure also returns to zero after a minute away from the app or on a fresh launch.
Switch camera
Tap the camera button beside the flash to switch between the front and rear cameras.
Shutter
Tap for a photograph. Hold for a moment to start a video: the shutter pulses and becomes a red square. Release your finger to keep recording; tap the red square to stop. A clip keeps the orientation and glitch direction it started with. Leaving the camera or an interruption ends the recording. On iOS, video records sound when microphone access is allowed; otherwise it is silent. After the first permission prompt, hold the shutter again to record. The current Android alpha records silent video.
Text size
Interface text follows your phone’s system text size setting. If the mode buttons no longer fit, swipe their row sideways. At accessibility text sizes, Custom opens in a separate panel.
