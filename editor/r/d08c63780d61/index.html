Open a photograph, explore effects with Random or choose a preset, then keep a moment with Pause and Save frame. Saving creates a separate PNG and leaves your original untouched.

      
Processing and privacy

      
WebGPU uses your device’s graphics processor, and WebAssembly runs image processing in your browser. Your photographs and effect settings stay on your device. They are not sent to the developer or uploaded for processing. No account, analytics or tracking scripts are included.

      
Once the editor has finished loading, you can keep editing and saving without an internet connection. Reopening the editor offline is not supported yet. Closing or reloading the page clears the current photo and unsaved edit.

      
The website host receives ordinary page requests to deliver the editor, such as your IP address. Those requests do not contain your photographs. Optional support links open external services.

      
Read the web editor’s privacy policy.

      
Melt finds an edge and lets it flow downward.

      
Random is selected when you first open the editor. Open a photo to start exploring, or choose a preset or Custom. Random follows the phone editor’s recipe journeys. Custom keeps the displayed mix for manual adjustment. Pause and Save hold the shown frame; Continue in Random starts a fresh journey from it.

      
Speed changes how quickly Random and live effects move. 80% is the standard pace; use Pause to hold a frame. Exposure adjusts the photo before effects. Add text with Text, then drag its outline or adjust its size and angle. Text is processed along with the photo. Presets and Reset effects keep your exposure and text; Original shows the untouched photo.

      
Photos are opened as a working copy, up to 720 pixels on the shorter side and 2048 on the longer side. PNG exports use that working resolution. Video and saved custom slots are not available in this web version yet.
