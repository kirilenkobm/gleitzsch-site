Gleitzsch photo editor

Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. The paperwork goes here. Because compliance and regulation are the new AI.

Last updated November 22, 2026.

This page covers the Gleitzsch browser photo editor, maintained by Bogdan Kirilenko. Provider and postal contact details: Impressum. The separate project policy covers the iOS/Android apps and project website.

Your photographs

Photographs are opened and processed in your browser using WebAssembly and WebGPU on your device. Your images, text, effect settings and rendered frames are not uploaded to the developer or sent to a server for processing. No account is required. This editor includes no advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic crash-reporting scripts. The text font is bundled with the editor; it does not contact a font service.

The editor works with a reduced copy in memory. It leaves the original file untouched. Saving downloads a new PNG from the displayed frame. The PNG includes the Gleitzsch name, app version and project website in its metadata. It does not copy the original photograph’s location or camera metadata, or add an author or user/device identifier. Your browser and operating system manage the downloaded file and any cloud synchronization you have chosen.

The current photo and edit are not saved automatically in browser storage. Closing or reloading the page discards them. The browser may cache the editor’s program files; those files do not contain your photographs. No service worker or persistent offline installation is included. Once loaded, the editor can continue processing and saving without a network connection; reopening it offline is not yet supported.

Website requests

This editor can be served through Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives ordinary technical requests needed to deliver the editor’s HTML, code and resources, including your IP address. These requests do not include your photographs, text or effect settings. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies to Cloudflare hosting; GitHub’s privacy statement applies to GitHub Pages hosting. This is separate from local image processing.

Links to the project showcase, GitHub Issues, Telegram, support and Impressum open external services with their own privacy practices. The editor does not attach photos to feedback automatically.

Optional diagnostics and support

About → Technical details → Save diagnostics downloads a JSON file to your device. It contains the browser/GPU description, app/build versions, processing parameters, exposure, number of text labels, working image dimensions, frame hashes and error messages. It does not contain image pixels or the text you entered and is not sent automatically. Review it before choosing to share it.

For support or privacy questions, email gleitzsch@mail.online-impressum.de. If you send a message or attach a file, the developer receives what you choose to send and uses it to handle the request. The support mailbox is supplied by Online-Impressum (Clear-Media UG (haftungsbeschränkt)) and its contracted hosting providers and is read through WebMail. Support messages are not automatically forwarded to an AI assistant. Please avoid sending personal information unrelated to your request. Public reports posted through GitHub Issues are public; use email for private requests.

Legal bases, retention and your rights

The developer processes technical delivery requests and voluntarily supplied support information on the basis of legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: delivering and protecting the editor, responding to requests and investigating reported problems. Particular records required by law are handled under Article 6(1)(c). The editor does not require you to send a photograph to the developer, create an account or submit diagnostics.

Ordinary support emails and their attachments are scheduled for automatic deletion when more than 30 weeks (210 days) old, across all mailbox folders, including received and sent messages and trash. This period concerns each message’s age, rather than the closure of a support request, and allows related follow-up and problem investigation. Unneeded attachments may be removed earlier. Where an erasure request must be granted, we do not wait for routine automatic deletion. Necessary records subject to a specific legal obligation or actual legal claim are kept separately for as long as that purpose requires. Provider logs and backups have their own retention and deletion cycles. Cloudflare and GitHub operate internationally, including in the United States.

The project policy describes recipients and international transfer safeguards. It also explains retention criteria and your rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, applicable portability and objection, how to contact us and how to complain to a data protection authority. These provisions apply to this editor and its support as well.

This policy will be updated if the editor’s data practices change.
