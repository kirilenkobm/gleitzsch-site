Privacy policy

Your photos stay on your device. The paperwork goes here. Because compliance and regulation are the new AI.

This policy covers Gleitzsch for iOS and Android, the browser photo editor and this project website, maintained by Bogdan Kirilenko.

Provider and postal contact details: Impressum.

Android is available as an early APK alpha. Some features described below are still iOS-only; the Android alpha records silent video and does not request microphone access.

Browser photo editor

The web editor processes a photograph, entered text and effect settings locally with WebGPU and WebAssembly. They are not uploaded. Saving downloads a separate PNG with the Gleitzsch name, version and project website in its metadata; the original file is unchanged. There is no account, analytics or automatic project saving. Closing or reloading discards the current edit. The browser may cache the program files, which do not contain photographs. The hosting provider handles ordinary requests to deliver those files, as described below.

Read the web editor privacy policy for processing, offline use, downloads and optional diagnostics. The app-specific permissions and TestFlight details below apply to the native apps.

Image processing

Gleitzsch processes camera images, photographs you select and microphone audio on your device. The app does not upload your images, audio or effect settings to the developer or to a server. It does not require an account. We add no advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. Platform services may handle diagnostics separately; the iOS TestFlight case is described below.

All image effects run on your device using conventional signal-processing algorithms. No AI, no neural networks. The app does not send images to an AI service or use them to train models.

Camera, microphone and photo library

Camera access provides the live preview and lets you create glitched photographs and videos. The app does not request location access.

On iOS, microphone access is requested when you first start a video. With permission, video includes the original microphone sound. Without permission, videos are silent. The current Android alpha does not request microphone access and records silent video.

Preview and photographs do not use the microphone. On iOS, microphone capture stops when video recording ends or you leave the camera. Audio is not saved separately from the video.

Gleitzsch receives the photograph you choose to open in the editor, processes a reduced copy in memory and leaves the original unchanged.

On iOS, you can choose a picture in Apple’s photo picker or share it through Edit in Gleitzsch. Apple may download the selected item from iCloud Photos. A temporary local copy of the selected file is used while opening it and removed after import.

On iOS, saving a result requests add-only access to Photos. This permits adding a new photograph or video; it does not grant general read access. On Android, saving adds a photograph or video to the device’s media library without granting access to the rest of your library.

Editor results are new PNG images made from the displayed pixels, without copying the source photo’s location or device metadata. Your photo library or gallery manages saved photographs and videos. It may sync them through your chosen cloud photo service if you have enabled that service; Gleitzsch does not perform that sync.

You can change permissions in your device’s system settings.

On iOS, saved photo and video file metadata includes the Gleitzsch app name, version and project website. On Android, camera photographs and PNG editor results include the same app name, version and project website metadata. Gleitzsch does not add author, location or user/device identifiers. Sharing services may remove this metadata.

Local settings and saved mixes

C1–C3 mixes are stored locally. The camera remembers which favorite was active. Reset clears the current effect settings. Saved slots remain until you replace or delete them. On iOS, these local settings may be included in device backups managed by the operating system. The current Android port disables backup and device transfer for its private app data.

Deleting a mix does not delete photographs or videos already saved to your photo library. Manage those files in your photo library or gallery.

TestFlight and Apple diagnostics (iOS)

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you submit is also shared and may include your name, email address and attachments. This is separate from the app’s on-device image processing.

See TestFlight & Privacy. The developer uses beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate problems and improve Gleitzsch, and does not share them with third parties. Any copies retained by the developer are kept only as long as needed for this purpose or to meet legal obligations. Apple manages its own retention under its terms.

The privacy policy is ready. Permission to actually have a beta remains a thrilling administrative mystery.

Support and public issues

For support or privacy questions, email gleitzsch@mail.online-impressum.de. If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments through the email services used to deliver and receive your message and uses them to answer your request. The support address is provided by Online-Impressum. The in-app support draft includes the app and operating system versions; you can edit it before sending. Photographs and videos are not attached automatically. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

For requests to access or delete correspondence and beta feedback held by the developer, use the same email address.

Public bug reports and ideas can be posted through GitHub Issues. If you post an issue, your GitHub profile, message and attachments are public. Only share material you intend to publish. A GitHub account is required to post; it is not required to use the app.

Issue content is used to answer questions and investigate bugs and may remain as a public project record after an issue is closed. GitHub manages its storage and account data under its privacy statement. Use email for private support and privacy requests. TestFlight testers can also submit feedback through TestFlight.

This website

This static website can be served through GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded social feeds, third-party fonts, forms, cookies or browser storage set by this site.

When served through Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare receives technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and editor files. These requests do not contain your photographs, entered text or effect settings. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies to that hosting. When served through GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information to deliver the pages and files. APK downloads remain hosted on GitHub Pages, including when reached from the Cloudflare site. Downloading the APK does not register you for Google Play testing or create a Gleitzsch account. The developer does not collect APK download statistics. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that hosting. The Impressum link opens an external page hosted by Online-Impressum; its privacy policy applies there. Following a link to GitHub, Telegram, Apple or another service takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies this version.
